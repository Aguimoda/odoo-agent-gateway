# Discounts

<!-- Demo company handbook. Fictional content written for this project. -->

## What a salesperson can offer

A salesperson may give up to 10 percent discount on a quotation without asking anyone.

## Larger discounts

Any discount above 10 percent needs approval from a sales manager before the quotation is sent. Record the approval and its reason in the order's internal note.

## What never to do

Never promise a discount in an email or a call before it is approved, and never combine a discount with free shipping on the same order without the sales manager's agreement.
