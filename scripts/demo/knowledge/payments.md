# Payments and invoices

<!-- Demo company handbook. Fictional content written for this project. -->

## Payment terms

Invoices are due 30 days after the invoice date. New customers pay their first order in advance by bank transfer.

## Overdue invoices

When an invoice is 15 days overdue, accounting sends a reminder. After 45 days overdue, new orders for that customer are blocked until the balance is paid.

## Accepted methods

Bank transfer, card, and direct debit for customers with a signed mandate. We do not accept cash.
