# Returns

<!-- Demo company handbook. Fictional content written for this project. -->

## Who can return what

Customers may return a product within 30 days of delivery if it is unused and in its original packaging. Used, assembled or custom-made products cannot be returned unless they are faulty.

## How to register a return

Open the original sales order, use the Return action on its delivery, and choose the products and quantities coming back. Add the reason in the internal note. The warehouse checks the goods when they arrive.

## Refunds

Once the warehouse confirms the goods are in good condition, accounting issues a credit note. The refund reaches the customer within 5 working days, using the original payment method.
