# Shipping

Demo company handbook. Fictional content written for this project.

## Delivery times

Orders confirmed before 14:00 leave the warehouse the same day. Mainland Spain: 2 to 3 working days. Balearic Islands: 3 to 5 working days. Canary Islands, Ceuta and Melilla: 5 to 7 working days, plus customs paperwork handled by our carrier.

## Shipping costs

Shipping is free for orders over 300 euros before tax. Below that amount a flat fee of 15 euros applies. Heavy furniture delivered to an upper floor without a lift has a 40 euro surcharge.

## Damaged deliveries

If a parcel arrives damaged, the customer should write "damaged" on the carrier's delivery note and send us photos within 48 hours.
